MALENIA, BLADE OF MIQUELLA

" ...Heed my words. I am Malenia. Blade of Miquella. And I have never known defeat."


Deep within the haligtree's roots, shrouded in pale blossoms and the creeping stain of Scarlet Rot, Malenia stands as a quiet monument to tragic perfection. Though born an Empyrean—endowed with the sacred right to succeed the gods—her divine inheritance was marred from birth by an inescapable curse that eats away at her flesh. Defying the rot that sought to claim her, she bound her body with gold and prosthetic steel, transforming her agony into an fluid, lethal mastery of the blade that remains unmatched across the Lands Between.
