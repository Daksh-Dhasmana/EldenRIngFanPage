GODRICK THE GRAFTED

Perched high atop the cliffs of Limgrave in the windswept keep of Stormveil, Godrick the Grafted sits as a ruler over a rotting kingdom.
Though he carries the blood of the Golden Lineage—descended from the revered First Elden Lord—that divine heritage has thinned into something desperate and warped.
Obsessed with matching the legendary strength of his ancestors, Godrick turned to the grim, grotesque art of grafting, attaching the severed limbs of fallen warriors to his own frame in an unending pursuit of power.
