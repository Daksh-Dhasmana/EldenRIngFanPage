RENNALA, QUEEN OF THE FULL MOON

" Hush, little culver. I'll soon birth thee anew, a sweeting fresh and pure…


Enthroned within the grandeur of the Raya Lucaria Academy, Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon, sits suspended in a dreamlike twilight, a tragic remnant of an empire's former glory. Once a formidable master of lunar sorceries who brought the Golden Order to a standstill, her heart was utterly shattered when her lord Radagon abandoned her, leaving behind only a great amber egg. Now, lost in a haze of grief and obsession, she spends her days cradled in shadows, endlessly rebirthing frail, imperfect scholars while gazing longing into the pale light of the moon she once commanded.
