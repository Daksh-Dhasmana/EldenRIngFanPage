MORGOTT, THE OMEN KING

" Have it writ upon thy meagre grave: "Felled by King Morgott! Last of all kings."


Resting at the foot of the Erdtree as the true ruler of Leyndell, Morgott, the Omen King, stands as a tragic, self-appointed guardian of the very order that rejected him. Born a cursed Omen and hidden away in subterranean dark, he never succumbed to malice, choosing instead to love a Golden Order that deemed him an abomination. Wielding a cane that conceals a royal blade and commanding spectral weapons born of holy magic, he defends the capital's throne with fierce, tragic devotion—a true lord who slays all who dare reach for the Elden Ring, despite knowing he will never be embraced by its light.
