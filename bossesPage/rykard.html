RENNALA, QUEEN OF THE FULL MOON

" Join the Serpent King, as family... Together, we will devour the very gods!


Slumbering within the fiery depths of Mt. Gelmir, Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy, slithers as a monstrous testament to absolute ambition turned monstrous madness. Once a revered praetor and noble son of the Carian royal line, he willingly fed himself to an ancient, immortal serpent in a bid to acquire power vast enough to devour the gods themselves. Now merged into a grotesque entity of fangs, magma, and writhing flesh, he rules the volcano's dark hold—beckoning ambitious warriors to join his blasphemous horde, where all shall become one within the endless maw of the serpent.
